Seamanship

You cannot sail straight into the wind. Watch the pennant and the teal mark on the compass, and zigzag about 45° either side to go upwind. Red marks on the compass are enemy ships. Skeletons guard buried treasure. Sharks hunt swimmers in deep water. Restock planks and cannonballs at the crates on the Gullhaven pier.

MoveWASD or the left side of the screen. Drag to look. UseE or the action button: helm, anchor, cannons, repairs. FightClick, R or Strike; hold Block (right-click / V) to parry. Skeletons guard treasure, sharks hunt swimmers. BoardBring your ship alongside a rival and press E to swing aboard. Beat her crew for a bounty. Some rivals will try to board you too: your crew fights back. SailRaise the anchor at the bow, take the helm, raise sails, trim to the outline. UpwindZigzag about 45° off the wind. RepairsHold E at a hole to patch, hold B to bail. TreasureMatch the map to the chart, dig at the X, carry it home and sell it. The fortGullhaven's shore batteries work in every mode: walk behind a gun and press E. In Hold the Fort, sink raider ships and their rowboats before they land; the townsfolk fight anyone who reaches the beach. Rebuild a wrecked battery with a plank. WeaponsTap the weapon or Food chips at the top left to switch or eat. Hold Z (or the weapon button) for the item wheel.
